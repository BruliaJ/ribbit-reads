# Read first, every session

- **docs/HANDOFF.md**: what this game is, how it is built, and Julia's standing decisions. Read it before any work.
- Sister projects of Numberoo (`BruliaJ/math-game`), Panda Hanzi (`BruliaJ/hanzi-game`) and Loopaloo: same owner, same standards.

## Julia's standing rules (short version)
- Julia is not a developer: short, direct answers; one question at a time.
- Best long-term solution every time; kids should find it really fun, with no bugs.
- Kids' apps law first: COPPA, Apple Kids Category, Google Families. Nothing invasive about kids.
- **Never store or use a child's real name.** Kids may type or say a name to learn to spell it; it is never saved.
- **No microphone in the apps or on the website.** Asking Pip by voice exists only inside Claude (Claude dictation)
  and must stay out of any store build. Pip speaking uses the device's own voice only (never online voices).
- Nothing is sent anywhere: no analytics, ads, accounts, or outside requests (fonts ship with the game).
- Effort praise only; original characters only; never re-lock pals; never take earned progress away.
- Follow the Heggerty phonemic-awareness skill order, but never copy Heggerty (or any curriculum's) materials.
- Work on a branch, open a PR, merge once CI is green. Commits authored by thejulia@gmail.com.
